# Surface: index.html (Kevin Ou personal site)

Mode: Experience. This is a one-page personal site, and the journal itself leads from the first viewport.
Audience: admissions officers, program reviewers, and peers, often arriving from an Instagram bio on a phone.
Job: meet Kevin, browse his range, and reach him (LinkedIn, GitHub, email, three Instagram accounts).
User pins: a junk journal fused with a Japanese station stamp-rally book, one page, a cool cursor trail, and everything interactive.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is Kevin's junk journal, a stamp-rally book. Every part of his life is a station you collect by pressing its stamp. It refuses the category default of a hero, an about section, and experience cards.

OWN-WORLD:
- Ground: indigo bookcloth.
- Pages: torn papers layered on top: blue grid, kraft, mint rail-ticket stock, ledger pink, and photocopy grey.
- Fixings: washi tape in yellow, coral, and blue stripes.
- Inks: eki-stamp red is reserved for anything you can touch. Stamp indigo and green carry the station stamps.
- Type: Dela Gothic One for stamp and display lettering, Zen Maru Gothic for body text, Gaegu for handwritten margin notes, and Courier Prime for ticket data.
- Structure: no cards, and no boxes. Grouping comes from overlap and tape.

STORY: The visitor learns that Kevin is one curious kid across business, diplomacy, music, scouting, code, and photos. They believe it because the facts are exact. They collect stamps, poke at the objects, and end on the postcard with his links.

FIRST VIEWPORT: The opened journal cover-page spread fills the screen. 'Kevin Ou' is set huge in Dela Gothic over taped scraps. A mint rail-route strip runs along the top, listing the stations as links. A stamp-rally card at the right shows nine empty stamp rings, and the first stamp, HELLO, is pressable. Find-me links are on a taped ticket within the fold.

FORM: The user steered toward the Tour Stamp Book pick, blended with a junk journal: the pick (#1 on my ordered list) was fused with the junk journal. seed:dd205181ffc6a000. Raises kept from the round: one red reserved for touchable things; dropped scraps snap to a hidden module; items slide out of pockets to go deeper; picking up one item dims the rest.

SIGNATURE INTERACTION:
- A stamp rally. Each station's eki stamp presses with an ink-spread, fills its ring on the rally card, and when all are complete a paper-confetti send-off plays.
- Cursor trail: a red dashed rail line follows the pointer and fades, and clicking leaves a tiny stamp impression.
- Motion grammar: stamp-press (a quick scale-down plus ink bloom), paper lift on grab, and nothing else drifting.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
