## Platform

web

## Stack

Plain static HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with no framework and no build step. It is hosted on GitHub Pages from the repo `KevinOU22/KevinOU22.github.io` and served at https://kevinou22.github.io.

## Users

There is a mix of visitors. All of them look Kevin up from LinkedIn, Instagram, or a link he shares, and they want to know who he is in a minute or two:

- college admissions officers and summer-program reviewers
- internship, research-program, and competition judges
- friends and peers

## Product Purpose

This is Kevin Ou's personal website. It is one page that shows who he is: a Valley Christian High School student (class of 2028) who builds businesses, leads clubs, plays percussion, takes photos, and codes. Success means a visitor leaves remembering him as a specific person and can reach him through LinkedIn, Instagram, GitHub, or email.

## Positioning

The site works like a personal scrapbook rather than a resume template. His range is wide: DECA business plans, Model UN leadership, Scouts, a symphony tour of Japan, nonprofits, coding projects, and photography. That range is the point, so it is shown as collected pieces of a life, not as a list.

## Operating Context

- It is often opened on a phone from an Instagram bio or a LinkedIn profile, and sometimes on a laptop by a reviewer.
- It is one page: no multi-page navigation, and the whole story sits in one scroll.

## Capabilities and Constraints

These are requirements the user stated:

- one page
- a custom cursor trail that feels cool
- a scrapbook aesthetic
- interaction everywhere: most elements should react to hover, click, or drag

Constraints:

- Desktop and phone both have to work. Touch devices have no cursor, so the trail and hover effects need touch equivalents or graceful absence.
- No phone number or home address on the public site.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Kevin Ou.
- Voice: plain, friendly, first person, and short. He is a high school student, so avoid corporate puffery.

## Product Principles

1. Show range as one person. Every activity belongs to the same curious, hands-on kid, so don't split him into a resume grid.
2. Facts stay exact. Awards, dates, and numbers come straight from the resume, and nothing is inflated.
3. Play rewards attention. Interactions should reveal more about Kevin, not just decorate.
4. Reachable in one tap. The links are always easy to find, especially on a phone.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: the cursor trail and big motion turn off or calm down.
- Content stays readable and navigable by keyboard, and no fact is locked behind a hover-only interaction.
